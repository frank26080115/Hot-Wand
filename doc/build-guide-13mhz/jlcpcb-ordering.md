## PCB specifications

- Base material: FR-4
- Layers: 2
- Thickness: 1.6 mm
- Outer copper weight: 2 oz

The 2 oz copper is intentional. Keep the other fabrication options at their defaults.

## PCBA specifications

Select Standard PCBA and bottom-side assembly only. The top-side and omitted parts are installed by hand. Enable **Confirm Parts Placement** so component orientations can be checked before production.
